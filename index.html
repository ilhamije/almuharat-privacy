Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Al Maharat ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with us through WhatsApp, our customer service systems, or related channels.

When you message us on WhatsApp or interact with our automated systems, we may collect:

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of messages you send us
  • Order, appointment, or service-related information you share with us
  • Metadata such as message timestamps and delivery status

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process orders, bookings, or service requests
  • Send you updates, confirmations, or notifications related to your requests
  • Improve our services and internal operations

3. How We Share Your Information

Your information is processed using the following third-party services, which act as data processors on our behalf:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform — used to send and receive messages
  • Odoo — our business management and CRM system, used to store customer and order records
  • n8n — a workflow automation tool used to coordinate messages between our systems
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the information below. Upon a verified deletion request, we will remove your data from our active systems within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

6. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your data, please contact us at the email address below with your WhatsApp phone number and a description of your request. We will confirm once your data has been removed from our systems.
